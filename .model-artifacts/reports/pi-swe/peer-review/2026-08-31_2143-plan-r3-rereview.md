### Medium — rollback cannot restore overwritten targets from hashes alone

Affected: `04.03-atomic-completion-transition.md:42,64`.

The protocol writes new temps and atomically renames them over both targets, while the journal stores only pre/new hashes. Once a target is replaced, a pre-hash cannot restore its prior bytes. This conflicts with the promised deterministic rollback and `retain/restore pre-transition files` behavior, especially on second-write or post-write validation failure.

Action: either specify durable target-adjacent preimage/backup files and their fsync/cleanup/recovery ordering, or narrow the invariant to guaranteed roll-forward while both validated new temps exist and define an explicit blocked/manual-recovery state when they do not. Extend fault injection through every journal stage, including post-rename validation failure.

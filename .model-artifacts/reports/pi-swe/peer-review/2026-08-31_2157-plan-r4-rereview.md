## Findings

### Medium — journal stage machine and validated cleanup recovery are not defined

Affected: `.model-artifacts/plans/pi-swe/canonical-planning/revisions/r4/phases/04-workflow-rollout/04.03-atomic-completion-transition.md:49-52,60`

The journal has a `stage`, advances after each rename, and later becomes `validated`, but the contract does not define a closed stage enum or the target/prepared/backup invariants and recovery action for each stage. In particular, a crash after `validated` while cleanup has removed only some backups/prepared files is ambiguous: the broad recovery rule requires all backups and otherwise enters `blocked-recovery`, even though validated canonical new state should be authoritative and cleanup should resume idempotently.

Action: define a closed stage machine (for example `prepared`, `contract-index-installed`, `manifest-installed`, `validated`, `cleanup`) with exact per-stage invariants. Before `validated`, restore both verified preimages; at/after `validated`, verify canonical new target hashes/completion record and finish idempotent cleanup without rollback, tolerating already-removed cleanup artifacts. Add stage-boundary crash tests.

### Medium — completion records lack disposition and next-state cross-invariants

Affected: `04.03-atomic-completion-transition.md:41-46`

The closed record validates key/path/revision/evidence identity but does not require the indexed contract for `completionRecords[id]` to have status `complete`. It also does not fully require `nextState` dispositions to agree with the resulting contract index/manifest. A pending, blocked, or in-progress contract could therefore carry a syntactically valid completion record, undermining canonical-reader idempotency.

Action: reject records with absent keys or indexed status other than `complete`; validate `nextState.initiativeState`, active contract, and ready IDs against the same index/manifest snapshot; cover pending/blocked/in-progress record fixtures and mismatched next-state projections.

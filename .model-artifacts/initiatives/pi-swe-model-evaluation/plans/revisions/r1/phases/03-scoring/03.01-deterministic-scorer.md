## Non-goals

No live session execution, subjective code-quality grading, or policy mutation.

## Entry inputs

## Specialist decisions incorporated

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-03a: Score execution start, exact contract order, actual verification commands/exit codes, valid evidence chains, completion calls/statuses, blocker response, and final reconciliation.
- AC-03b: Separate model-chosen `swe_complete` retries, Pi transient retries, and harness trial reruns.
- AC-03c: Any approved immutable spec/plan/contract mutation is a critical violation; expected completion-record/status/active-contract mutations are not.
- AC-03d: Artifact prose cannot satisfy verification without correlated successful command evidence.
- AC-03e: Same inputs serialize to byte-identical score output.

## Verification

## Rollback
